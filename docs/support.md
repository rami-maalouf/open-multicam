# OpenMulticam support

OpenMulticam is a free camera app for iPhone and Android by Rami Maalouf, supported by an optional one-time donation. All recording features are available without paying.

## Contact

[Open a support request on GitHub](https://github.com/rami-maalouf/open-multicam/issues/new). Include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. GitHub issues are public. Do not attach private recordings or sensitive information.

## Recording

Allow camera and microphone access when prompted. The camera picker shows the configurations your device can run. Available camera pairs vary by hardware. On Android, two-camera modes need Android 11 or later and a device that supports concurrent cameras. Single-camera recording remains available when a pair is unsupported. Recording supports 1080p H.264 at the frame rates shown by the app.

Open Library to find your takes. Open a take to play, rename, export, or delete it. A recording with two separate camera files has two clips; check both clips when exporting.

## Permissions

If camera or microphone access was denied, open iOS Settings, choose Apps, then OpenMulticam, and enable the permission. On Android, open Settings, choose Apps, then OpenMulticam, then Permissions. Save to Photos on iOS requests permission to add videos only when you choose that action. Save to gallery on Android 11 and later needs no library permission; Android 10 and earlier ask for storage write access. Sharing to Files or another app uses the system share sheet.

## Storage and interrupted recordings

Export important takes and verify the exported videos before deleting the originals. Deleting the app removes its local library. Copies exported to Photos, your gallery, or Files remain in their destinations. Recordings may stop when the app goes into the background, the device overheats, or storage is exhausted. Reopen Library after an interruption to check for a recovered take.

## Donations

Settings > Support OpenMulticam contains an optional one-time donation billed by Apple on iOS or by Google Play on Android. It is not a subscription and does not unlock camera features. Use Restore Purchase with the same Apple Account or Google Account to restore the thank-you status on another device. If donations are temporarily unavailable, the camera remains fully usable. For billing or refund requests, use [Apple's purchase support](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) or [Google Play's refund help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Privacy

Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md). The released app has no advertising or analytics and does not upload your recordings to an app-operated server.
