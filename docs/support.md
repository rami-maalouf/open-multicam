# OpenMulticam support

OpenMulticam is a free iPhone camera app by Rami Maalouf, supported by an optional one-time donation. All recording features are available without paying.

## Contact

[Open a support request on GitHub](https://github.com/rami-maalouf/open-multicam/issues/new). Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. GitHub issues are public. Do not attach private recordings or sensitive information.

## Recording

Allow camera and microphone access when prompted. The camera picker shows the configurations your iPhone can run. Available camera pairs vary by hardware. Single-camera recording remains available when a pair is unsupported. Recording supports 1080p H.264 at the frame rates shown by the app.

Open Library to find your takes. Open a take to play, rename, export, or delete it. A recording with two separate camera files has two clips; check both clips when exporting.

## Permissions

If camera or microphone access was denied, open iOS Settings, choose Apps, then OpenMulticam, and enable the permission. Export to Photos requests permission to add videos only when you choose that action. Sharing to Files or another app uses the system share sheet.

## Storage and interrupted recordings

Export important takes and verify the exported videos before deleting the originals. Deleting the app removes its local library. Copies exported to Photos or Files remain in their destinations. Recordings may stop when the app goes into the background, the phone overheats, or storage is exhausted. Reopen Library after an interruption to check for a recovered take.

## Donations

Settings > Support OpenMulticam contains an optional one-time donation billed by Apple. It is not a subscription and does not unlock camera features. Use Restore Purchase with the same Apple Account to restore the thank-you status on another device. If donations are temporarily unavailable, the camera remains fully usable. For billing or refund requests, use [Apple's purchase support](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Privacy

Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md). The App Store app has no advertising or analytics and does not upload your recordings to an app-operated server.
