# OpenMulticam privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

## Your recordings stay with you

OpenMulticam is made by Rami Maalouf. Your recordings stay on your device until you export or share them. The app does not collect your name, contact details, photo library, advertising identifiers, or precise location, and there is no account or app-operated server. Camera and microphone input is processed on your device.

## Crash and performance reports

To find and fix problems, the app sends anonymous crash reports, error reports, and performance measurements to [Expo Observe](https://docs.expo.dev/eas/observe/introduction/), a service of Expo, the company whose tools OpenMulticam is built with. A report contains how long the app took to start and to open each screen, the screen's name, error messages with the place in the app's code where they happened, and device details such as the model, system version, language, app version, and battery, temperature, and network state. Each report carries a random ID that the app creates when it is installed. The ID is not linked to your identity and usually changes when you reinstall the app. Expo also receives the IP address of each report and keeps the country it came from. Reports do not include your recordings, take names, or anything you type. Expo keeps them for about 90 days, processes them only on our behalf, and does not use them for advertising or tracking ([Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)). Because reports are not tied to you, they cannot be looked up by your name, and they are deleted automatically. Apart from these reports, the app sends nothing from your device unless you donate, open a link, or export a recording.

## Camera, microphone, and exports

Camera access provides preview and video recording. Microphone access adds sound to your videos. On iOS, Add Photos access is requested only when you choose Save to Photos. On Android 11 and later, Save to gallery adds the selected videos without requesting library access; Android 10 and earlier ask for storage write access. OpenMulticam does not browse your existing photo library. You can change permissions in your device settings.

## Storage and deletion

Videos and recording details are stored in the app's local library until you delete them or remove the app. Your device's backup settings may include app data in a backup managed by your platform provider. Deleting a take from OpenMulticam does not delete copies you previously exported to Photos, your gallery, Files, or another app.

## Sharing is your choice

Recordings leave the app only when you choose to export or share them. The destination app or service then handles that copy under its own privacy policy. Your gallery may sync exported videos to a cloud service if you have enabled it.

## Optional donations

Apple or Google Play processes the optional one-time donation through your app store. OpenMulticam checks verified purchase status on your device to show a thank-you message and restore your purchase. We do not receive your payment card details or send purchase receipts to an app-operated server. Donating does not unlock features.

## Support and external pages

Opening support or source-code links takes you to GitHub, which applies its own privacy policy. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Please do not include private recordings or sensitive information.
