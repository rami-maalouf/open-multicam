Why it exists

The cameras are already in your pocket. The app is free.

An iPhone can run two of its cameras at once: the view and the person who sees it, or a wide shot and a close one. OpenMulticam opens that up to everyone, with no paywall, no account and nothing to hide in the code.

01

Free, all of it

Every camera feature is free. No subscription, no paywall. An optional one-time tip, if you want to give one.

02

Private

No account, ads or analytics. Your takes stay on your iPhone until you export or share them.

03

Open source

The whole app is on GitHub: an Expo app around a native Swift capture core. Read it, learn from it, fork it.
